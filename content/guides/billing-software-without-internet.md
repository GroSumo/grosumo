Offline billing software is installed on a PC in your shop and keeps your items, customers, bills and accounts in a local database on that same computer, so making a bill never has to wait for the internet. Power cuts and patchy mobile data are part of running a shop in many parts of India, and software that depends on a connection tends to stop at exactly the wrong moment: when there's a queue at the counter. Running everything locally avoids that.

## What works without internet

Everything you do at the counter and in the back office runs on the shop PC:

- **GST billing.** Make bills with CGST, SGST or IGST worked out for you, print them, and keep the invoice numbers in sequence.
- **Stock.** Stock levels update with every sale, purchase and return, so you always know what is on the shelf and what it is worth.
- **Customer credit.** Record credit sales and part payments, and see who owes you what, for how long.
- **Purchases and expenses.** Enter supplier bills and daily expenses as they happen.
- **Reports.** Sales, profit, stock and GST reports are worked out from the data on the PC, so they open instantly.

Because nothing has to travel to a server and back, the counter is often faster offline than with cloud software on a slow connection.

## What needs an internet connection

Only a few things need a connection, and none of them stop you billing:

- **Sharing on WhatsApp.** Sending a bill, statement or payment reminder on WhatsApp needs internet at the moment you send it. It's optional, and it only sends that one document — your business data stays on your PC.
- **Downloading updates.** New versions are downloaded over the internet; you choose when (see below).
- **Getting help remotely.** If our team helps you on a screen-sharing call, that call needs a connection.

A basic mobile hotspot is enough for all three.

## Backups: the one thing to get right

When your data lives on one machine, backups are what protect it. A PC can fail, be damaged or be stolen, so plan for it before it happens:

1. **Automatic daily backups.** The grosumoERP Offline edition takes a local backup every day, without anyone having to remember.
2. **A copy off the PC.** Copy the latest backup to a pen drive or a second disk regularly — weekly is a good habit — and keep it somewhere other than the counter.
3. **Know how to restore.** A backup is only useful if it can be put back. If you ever move to a new PC, the team can help you restore your latest backup onto it.

A few minutes a week on backups is the difference between a bad day and losing years of records.

## How updates arrive with AMC

The Offline edition software is free, forever. The ₹1,999 per year AMC (annual maintenance contract) covers three things:

- **Updates.** New versions with fixes, improvements and changes such as new GST requirements.
- **Support.** Help from the grosumo team on WhatsApp and phone.
- **Licence renewal.** Your yearly licence stays active.

When an update is available, you download and install it while the PC is connected — ideally at a quiet time, not during the evening rush. Take a backup before installing, as you would before any software change. If you'd rather not do it yourself, the team can guide you through it on a call.

If you don't renew the AMC, the software keeps working and your data stays yours on your PC. You miss new updates and support, and after a grace period, features that depend on an active licence pause until you renew. Your bills and records are never locked away from you.

## Switching to cloud later

Offline is a good place to start: it costs the least and needs nothing more than a Windows PC. If your needs change — say you want to check sales from home, add a second billing device, or open another branch — you can move to the Cloud edition (₹9,999 per year, all-in). The team helps you move your data across, so you keep your items, customers and history. The modules are the same in both editions, so there's nothing new to learn.

## Is offline right for your shop?

Offline is a strong fit if:

- your internet is unreliable or you bill during power-backup hours
- you bill from a single counter PC
- you want your business data to stay on your own machine
- you want the lowest yearly cost

If you mostly want to see your numbers from anywhere, compare it with the Cloud edition before you decide.
