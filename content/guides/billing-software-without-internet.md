Power cuts and patchy mobile data are part of running a shop in many parts of India. Billing software that depends on the internet stops working at exactly the wrong moment: a queue at the counter. Offline billing software avoids that by running entirely on your own computer.

## What "offline" really means

Offline billing software is installed on a PC in your shop. Your items, customers, bills and accounts are stored in a database on that same computer. When you make a bill, nothing needs to reach the internet, so the counter keeps moving even when the connection drops.

## What still works without internet

- Making GST bills and printing them
- Updating stock with every sale and purchase
- Recording customer credit and payments
- Sales, profit, stock and GST reports

## When you do need a connection

- **Software updates.** Downloading a new version needs internet, but you can do it when it suits you.
- **WhatsApp sharing.** Sending a bill or payment reminder on WhatsApp needs a connection at the moment you send it.

## Backups: the one thing to get right

Because your data lives on one machine, backups matter. Look for software that:

1. Backs up automatically every day, without you remembering to do it
2. Lets you copy backups to a pen drive or second disk
3. Can restore a backup onto a new PC

The grosumoERP Offline edition takes an automatic local backup every day.

With grosumoERP, the Offline edition software is free, forever. You pay only a ₹1,999 per year AMC, which covers updates, support and licence renewal. If you later want to reach your data from anywhere, you can move to the Cloud edition (₹9,999 per year) and take your data with you.
