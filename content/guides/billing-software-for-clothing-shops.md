## Setting up your garment catalogue

### Sizes and colours as variants

grosumoERP has three item types: **Standard** (one SKU), **Variant** (one design, many SKUs), and **Service** (no stock — alterations, stitching). For garments you'll mostly use Variant.

You create the parent once — "Men's Checked Shirt, Blue", with its category, GST rate and HSN code — then choose attributes. Size, Colour, Fit, Material, Length, Waist and Pattern are ready to pick; you can add your own. grosumoERP generates every combination in one go, and each variant gets:

- its own SKU and barcode
- its own stock count, per location if you have more than one
- its own price and cost if needed (a 3XL that costs more, a clearance size)

GST, HSN and the reorder point come from the parent, so you set them once. Stock screens show the parent ("42 blue checked shirts") and the breakdown ("but only 2 in M") — which is the number that actually tells you what to reorder.

### GST on readymade garments

Set up categories the way you think about the shop — Men's Shirts, Trousers, Kids, Ethnic, Accessories. GST on readymade garments depends on the price per piece; the rate (0, 5, 12, 18 or 28%) goes on the garment, and every bill line carries it, splitting CGST/SGST or IGST by the customer's state. If you're on the composition scheme, that's supported too. Confirm the current slabs with your CA at setup, then it's automatic. For what a compliant bill must show, see the [GST invoice format guide](/guides/gst-invoice-format).

### Barcode labels for every piece

Most garment shops hand-write price tags or pay a printer for labels. grosumoERP generates a **Code128 barcode** for every product and variant and prints labels from the browser: sizes from 38×25 mm to 100×100 mm, or A4 sheets, with ready profiles for the Xprinter XP-365B and TSC TE244. When a purchase arrives, print labels for the whole delivery in one go. The same barcode scans at the counter.

## Billing at the counter

Scan the label and the right size and colour land on the bill; if the label's missing, search by name and pick the size. Per line you can give a percentage or flat discount; there's a bill-level discount too. Take payment by cash, UPI or card, split between cash and UPI, put it on the customer's credit with a due date, or redeem store credit or a voucher and collect the rest. A new customer is added with a name and phone number without leaving the bill.

The bill prints on A4 or an 80 mm thermal printer, with your logo and terms, or goes to the customer's WhatsApp with one tap.

### Billing on a phone

The **mobile view** on the shop Wi-Fi is a real billing screen, not a lookup tool: it sells standard and variant items with the size/colour picker, shows GST, takes cash, UPI, card or credit, and prints to a Bluetooth receipt printer on Android. It also shows today's sales, each customer's outstanding, expenses and the P&L. Returns, exchanges and vouchers stay at the desktop counter.

## Exchanges in one step

This is the feature clothing shops notice first. Without it, an exchange means a return against the old bill, a new sale, and working out the difference by hand — with a chance one of them gets done wrong.

With **Exchange** switched on, the cashier opens the original bill, marks the item coming back, adds the item going out, and submits. grosumoERP records the return and the new sale together. If the new item costs more, collect the difference by cash, UPI or card. If it costs less, the shop's side of the difference goes to the customer as **store credit** for their next visit. Stock moves correctly for both items and the books are right without anyone touching a journal.

Plain returns work the same way: partial quantities against the original bill, refund at the original price, by cash or bank or as store credit.

## Regulars: credit, store credit, vouchers and points

A garment shop's best customers come back for every function. grosumoERP gives you four ways to look after them, and all of them post to the accounts properly.

**Credit (udhaar).** A bill can be left pending with a due date. The customer's outstanding shows on their card and in the overdue list, and a WhatsApp reminder goes out with one tap when it's time.

**Store credit.** A wallet per customer, separate from their bills. It fills from a return without refund, the shop's side of an exchange, an advance payment, a goodwill gesture, or unspent voucher value — and it's spent at the counter in full or in part alongside cash, UPI or card. Advances, goodwill and return credit never expire.

**Gift vouchers.** Sell a voucher for cash before Diwali or a wedding season, or issue promotional ones free — in batches of up to 500. Each is a fixed amount with a barcode printed on an 80 mm slip; it's redeemed at the counter by typing or scanning the code, and anything unspent stays as store credit for that customer. Vouchers expire after a year by default, and the accounting — from sale, through redemption, to expiry — is handled for you.

**Loyalty points.** Points on purchases that convert to store credit.

## Seasons, slow stock and the money in it

Garment shops don't lose money on the bills they raise; they lose it on the stock that doesn't move. grosumoERP's insights rank every item as fast, slow, dead or overstocked (slow means no sale in 60 days), show how much capital is stuck per category, and list what needs reordering now. Before you buy for the next season, that tells you which sizes and colours actually sold last time. After the season, it tells you what to clear before it's a year old. Top- and low-margin products, your top customers, and a peak-hours heatmap are there too.

The day close tallies sales, payments by mode, refunds, cost of goods, expenses and net income for the day, and because the books are proper double-entry underneath, you get a P&L, balance sheet and trial balance your CA can use.

## Two outlets, or a godown

Each outlet or storeroom is a **location** with its own stock. **Stock transfers** move garments between them with a record of what went where, and stock adjustments carry a reason — damage, shrinkage, count correction. Costing is FIFO, so margins are real.

## Staff at the counter

Add users with roles. A cashier can bill and take returns but can't edit or void a sale, open the accounts, or see reports; every change is logged. If you pay sales commissions, turn on Partners & Commissions and record what's owed per person. Before a new cashier touches real bills, switch on **Training Mode**: the software works on a copy of your data for an hour, and your real books are untouched when you switch it off.

## How much you can customise

Every clothing shop runs a little differently, so this is the question owners ask most. There are five levels.

**1. Switches (no code).** Exchange, Quotations, Lists, Store credit, Vouchers, Loyalty, Partners & Commissions, Training Mode, GST itself — on or off. A shop that doesn't do vouchers never sees the screen.

**2. Custom fields (no code).** Add your own fields to products, customers, suppliers, employees, bills, bill lines, purchases and expenses — fabric, brand, supplier article number, a customer's usual size, an "alteration needed" flag on a line. They show on forms, list columns, the A4 invoice and the POS checkout.

**3. Lists — your own tables (no code).** For things the software doesn't model, define a simple table with columns — text, number, date, yes/no, or a link to a customer or staff member — and add rows. For a garment shop: an **alterations register** (customer, garment, measurements, promised date, delivered?), a **tailoring orders** list, a **uniform issue** register.

**4. Layouts and dashboard (no code).** An invoice designer for A4 and 80 mm — reorder sections, pick columns, colours and fonts — plus HTML templates for A4, A5 and thermal, with your logo and terms. Dashboard widgets — KPIs, charts, alerts, saved searches — are built by the admin and published per role.

**5. Built for you (grosumo).** For rules and new screens — block a sale below cost, require a customer on bills over ₹10,000, a nightly list of stock untouched for 90 days — grosumo writes scripts against grosumoERP's script engine. A real example: for one client, grosumo built an employee check-in/check-out screen with half-day leaves and a daily roster on top of Lists — no change to the core product, no payroll. Most shops never need this level; the ones that do get it as part of a services engagement.

**What you can't change:** the accounting underneath. Double-entry rules, GST calculation and FIFO costing are fixed on purpose — that's what keeps your books right.
