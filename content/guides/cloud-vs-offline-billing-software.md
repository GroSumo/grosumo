Every shop needs fast GST billing, accurate stock and a clear view of who owes what. The real question is **where the software runs**: on servers you reach over the internet (cloud), or on a computer in your shop (offline). Both can do the same job. They differ in cost, internet needs and how you reach your data.

## The short answer

- Choose **offline** if you bill from one counter PC, your internet is unreliable, or you want your data to stay on your own machine.
- Choose **cloud** if you want to check sales from home or on the move, use more than one device, or prefer that backups happen off-site automatically.

grosumoERP offers both, with the same modules in each: an Offline edition where the software is free and you pay ₹1,999 a year for AMC, and a Cloud edition at ₹9,999 a year, all-in.

## How offline billing software works

Offline software is installed on a Windows PC in your shop. Billing, stock and accounts all run on that computer, so a slow or dead internet connection never stops a sale.

**Good for:**

- Shops with patchy internet or frequent outages
- Owners who want their data to stay on their own PC
- Keeping yearly costs low

**Plan for:**

- Backups. A good offline app backs up automatically every day; you should also copy backups off the PC from time to time.
- Access. The data lives on that PC, so you see it when you are at the shop.

## How cloud billing software works

Cloud software runs on remote servers and you open it in a web browser. Your data is stored with the provider, so you can log in from any device.

**Good for:**

- Owners who want to check sales and dues from anywhere
- Shops with more than one billing device or location
- Hands-off backups and updates

**Plan for:**

- A working internet connection at the counter
- Choosing a provider that hosts data in India and backs it up automatically

| Access from anywhere | No | Yes |
| Backups | Local, on your PC | Automatic, off-site |
| grosumoERP price | Software free + ₹1,999/yr AMC | ₹9,999/yr all-in |

## Questions to ask before you choose

1. How reliable is the internet at your counter?
2. Do you need to see sales when you are away from the shop?
3. How many devices will bill at the same time?
4. Who will take care of backups?

If you are still unsure, start offline. It costs the least, and you can move to cloud later without losing your data.
